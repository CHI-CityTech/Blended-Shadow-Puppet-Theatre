## Summary

### Organza Project Improvements Discussion

The team discussed revisiting and improving the organza project, which previously used cardboard and string but now needs a more durable solution. David introduced new team members including Gabriel Aguilera, who will work on video server technology, and Sean, who will focus on music and audio integration this semester. The group plans to revisit the first two scenes and add a third scene written by Tashari, with Nazanin expressing confidence in making this work and noting that having the scene outline would help with sound design planning.

### Puppet Show Setup Improvements

David discussed improvements to a puppet show setup, including adding two feet of height for more space and plans to drop masking in the third scene to create three areas for content presentation. He explained their strategy to use virtual shadows in complex scenes like the dragon attacking the ship where they previously lacked sufficient personnel, and mentioned Sam's experiments with shadow puppets that respond to hand gestures using computer camera sensors. Chris was invited to present ideas about developing intermissions with different types of puppetry, potentially starting with a historically accurate 13th century presentation.

### Mamluk Puppetry Performance Planning

David discussed plans to incorporate traditional Mamluk puppets and shadow puppetry techniques into their performance, including using projected light sources and potentially adding fabric elements for texture. He mentioned a future opportunity to collaborate on staging an Arabic play in New York, though this would likely be a two-year project. The team agreed to explore using projection cloth and fabric pieces in their setup, with Nazanin suggesting ways to assign different pieces to students at various skill levels.

### Organza Scroll Design Development

David discussed his project involving an organza scroll with a horizontal design, focusing on preserving the fabric's integrity while allowing for scrolling through multiple scenes. Nazanin suggested making individual fabric pieces that can be attached to a base fabric, allowing for modularity while maintaining stability. They also considered using projection screen fabric as an alternative to organza, with Nazanin offering to provide a roll of projection fabric for experimentation. The team agreed to prioritize developing the mechanical scrolling system before finalizing the fabric and attachment methods.

### Shadow Puppet Design Discussion

The team discussed design decisions for their shadow puppet performance, focusing on whether mechanical components should be visible to audiences. David proposed options ranging from steampunk-style visible mechanisms to discreet hidden components, with the group considering using a scrim from the beginning to set audience expectations. The discussion also covered potential additions like fog machines, lighting effects, and Foley sound effects to enhance immersion, particularly for ocean scenes. Sean mentioned his interest in incorporating Foley techniques and musical composition with Gamelan influences, while the team noted they would need to consult with the playwright regarding the musical theme and setting.

David and Nazanin discussed the use of a new communication platform for the shadow puppet project, emphasizing the need to select one platform to avoid confusion.
